2. Description of Service

Maple Tracker is an independent personal tool for iOS and Android that helps users keep residence and travel records, estimate days of presence in Canada, practise citizenship questions and generate PDF reports. Features and backup options vary by platform. Estimates and automatic detection can be incomplete or inaccurate; review and correct your records.

Always verify your eligibility with IRCC or a qualified immigration professional before applying for citizenship.

4. User Data

Trip and residence records are stored on your device. On iOS, you can optionally synchronize them through your private iCloud account; location samples are included only when Location History Sync is separately enabled. Android has no iCloud or Maple Tracker cloud synchronization, but system backup or device transfer may include app data according to your device settings. You are responsible for checking your records and retaining any reports or backups you need.

5. Location Services

Automatic trip detection is optional and requires the relevant location permissions. On Android, country lookup may use the system geocoding provider over the network and is not guaranteed to be available or accurate. Background tracking uses a visible Android location service and requires background location and notification permissions. The ongoing notification and the app’s Settings let you stop tracking; you can also revoke permissions in device settings.

On Android, tracking can stop if permissions are revoked, the process is terminated, the app is force-stopped or the device restarts; reopening the app may be required. Manual trip entry remains available. The Privacy Policy describes platform-specific location, backup, analytics and deletion behavior.
