2. Description of Service

Maple Tracker is a personal tool designed to help users track their physical presence in Canada for the purpose of monitoring eligibility for Canadian citizenship. The App provides day counting, trip logging, residency tracking, citizenship quiz, and PDF report generation features. The App supports multiple languages and offers both Light and Dark display modes.

The App may request location access to detect trips automatically. Country detection is performed on your device. You can disable automatic tracking in Maple Tracker Settings or revoke location permission in iOS Settings.
