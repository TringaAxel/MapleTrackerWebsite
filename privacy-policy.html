1. Introduction

Axel Tringa ("we", "us", "our") operates Maple Tracker (the "App"). This policy explains how the iOS and Android versions handle information. Platform-specific features are identified below; iCloud synchronization is available on iOS, not Android.

2. Data Stored Locally

The App stores your trip history, residency dates, citizenship calculations, preferences, and settings on your device. If automatic trip detection is enabled, recent location samples may include latitude, longitude, timestamp, and detected country. Location history is limited and used to identify border crossings.

On iOS, you can enable the applicable iCloud synchronization options. On Android, system backup or device-to-device transfer can include app records and preferences according to your device settings. A restored backup may restore your previous usage-analytics choice.

3. iCloud and Android System Backup

On iOS, enabling iCloud sync synchronizes trip and residency data through your private Apple iCloud account. Location samples are included only when you separately enable Location History Sync in Settings. Apple manages this synchronization; Maple Tracker does not operate its own trip-history storage server.

The Android version has no iCloud or Maple Tracker cloud-sync feature. Android, Google or your device provider may back up app data or transfer it to another device when system backup or transfer is enabled. These services are controlled through your device settings, separately from the app.

4. Location Data

Location permission is optional; you can enter trips manually without it. With permission, the App uses the location available under your permission settings to identify your country and detect departures from and returns to Canada. Location samples are stored in app storage. The App does not attach raw latitude or longitude to its Firebase Analytics events; country-related event parameters are described below.

On Android, country lookup may pass latitude and longitude to the Android geocoding service, which may contact its provider over the network. This lookup is not guaranteed to remain entirely on the device. If you enable background access and the required notification permission, tracking can continue while the app is not in use. An ongoing Android notification shows the tracking session and provides a Stop action.

You can stop automatic tracking in the App or revoke location access in iOS or Android settings. On Android, a force-stop, process termination or device restart can interrupt tracking; reopen the App to resume it. On iOS, location samples may also be synchronized when both iCloud Sync and Location History Sync are enabled.

5. Firebase Services

We use Google Firebase for:

  • Push notifications: Firebase Messaging processes installation identifiers, a messaging registration token and app/configuration information. On Android, automatic registration can occur when Firebase initializes even if you decline usage analytics or notification display permission. The app stores its current messaging token locally; blocking visible notifications does not itself disable Firebase registration.
  • Optional usage analytics: Firebase Analytics receives feature interactions, app/device information and analytics identifiers when enabled. The SDK can derive approximate location from the device’s IP address. The app does not provide advertising features.

Usage analytics is off by default. You can enable it by accepting the onboarding analytics step or in Settings, and disable it later in Settings. Disabling Firebase Analytics also resets its local data and app-instance identifier; it does not delete events already transmitted to Firebase.

Android app events can include a trip’s country and duration, trip counts, partial-day and note-present flags, the pre-residence status selected during setup, quiz province/category/results, and language/theme choices. These events do not include trip-note text, exact residence dates or raw location coordinates.

PostHog is not configured in the Android 1.0 release and receives no events from that configuration. Where PostHog is configured in another version, it is controlled by its own optional consent and receives a limited set of feature-use events and analytics identifiers, without trip contents, coordinates, profile values or quiz answers.

6. Information You Choose to Provide

The App does not require your name, email address, immigration documents, financial information, contacts or photos to track your presence. No Maple Tracker account is required. If you email us, your email provider and the recipient receive the information you choose to send. Maple Tracker does not sell personal data.

7. Data Retention

You can edit or delete trips and reset the app’s profile, trip and location data. On Android, Reset All Data also disables usage analytics, but does not delete Firebase registration records, already uploaded analytics, generated PDF cache files, exported reports or system backups. Uninstalling normally removes local app storage; restoring a system backup can bring saved records and preferences back.

On iOS, synchronized data may remain in your private iCloud account until you use Reset Cloud Data or manage the App’s iCloud data through your Apple ID settings. On Android, manage system backups through your device’s backup controls. Firebase retains notification and analytics data under its applicable service and project retention settings. Local reset is not a promise to erase every remote copy.

PDF reports are generated on your device. Opening a report in another app gives that app access to the selected file; it handles saved or shared copies under its own policies. Resetting Maple Tracker does not delete exported copies. Website links open in your browser or an external handler, and feedback opens an email composer that you choose whether to send.

8. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple iCloud (optional iOS synchronization)
  • Google Firebase (notifications and optional analytics)
  • Android location/geocoding and system backup providers (country lookup and device-controlled backup or transfer)
  • PostHog, only where configured and enabled (optional feature-use analytics)

Providers and external apps have their own data-handling terms. The Android geocoding provider depends on the device; this policy does not assert that every provider retains data only in memory or that every system operation stays offline.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
