Maple Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Axel Tringa ("we", "us", "our") operates Maple Tracker (the "App"), available on iOS and Android. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

2. Data Stored Locally

The following data is stored exclusively on your device:

  • Trip history (departure/return dates, destinations)
  • Permanent residence date and arrival date
  • Citizenship day calculations
  • Profile preferences and settings
  • Display theme preference (Light or Dark)
  • Language preference

This data never leaves your device unless you enable iCloud sync (iOS only).

3. iCloud Sync (iOS)

If you enable iCloud sync on the iOS version, your trip and residency data is synced across your Apple devices via Apple's iCloud service. This data is encrypted and managed by Apple's iCloud infrastructure. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

The Android version does not include cloud synchronization: all data remains stored locally on your device.

4. Location Data

If you grant location permissions, the App uses your device's location to detect when you leave or return to Canada. Location data is:

  • Processed entirely on your device
  • Never sent to our servers or any third party
  • Used only to create trip records
  • Deletable at any time by revoking location permissions

5. Firebase Services

We use Google Firebase on both iOS and Android for:

  • Push notifications: To send you daily updates and reminders. Firebase receives a device token but no personal data.
  • Analytics: To understand general app usage patterns (screens visited, feature usage). This data is anonymized and aggregated.

You can opt out of analytics by disabling it in your device settings.

6. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email, or identity
  • Your immigration status or documents
  • Your exact GPS coordinates (only country-level detection)
  • Financial information
  • Contacts or photos

7. Data Retention

All personal data is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud sync on iOS, in your personal iCloud account. If you delete the App, all local data is permanently removed. iCloud data can be managed through your Apple ID settings.

8. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple iCloud (data sync, iOS only)
  • Google Firebase (notifications & analytics)

Each service has its own privacy policy that governs their handling of data.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at me@axeltringa.ca.
