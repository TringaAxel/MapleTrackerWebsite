How the count works

To apply for citizenship, you need 1,095 days of physical presence in Canada during the five years before you apply. Maple Tracker estimates your total from your dates and trips, using the rules below.

  • 1,095 days in five years

    Only the five years before your application count toward the total.

  • Time before PR counts half

    Each day spent in Canada as a temporary resident or protected person counts as half a day, up to 365 days.

  • Travel days count as present

    The day you leave and the day you return are both counted as days in Canada.

Maple Tracker provides an estimate and does not determine your eligibility. Check your dates against the official IRCC guidance before you apply.

Frequently asked questions

Is Maple Tracker an official IRCC app?

No. Maple Tracker is an independent personal tracking tool and is not affiliated with the Government of Canada. Always verify your eligibility and dates with IRCC before applying.

Does Maple Tracker tell me when I can apply?

It shows an estimate of your physical presence and a projected date. It does not assess the other citizenship requirements, so check the official IRCC guidance before you apply.

Is Maple Tracker available on Android?

Yes. Maple Tracker is on Google Play for Android 9 and later, and on the App Store for iPhone.

Does automatic trip detection send my location to a server?

No. Maple Tracker does not operate a server that receives your location. Your phone works out which country you are in; on Android, the system service that does this may use the network. On iPhone, location samples stay on the device unless you turn on both iCloud Sync and Location History Sync.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can begin tracking without providing a name, email address or immigration documents.

Can I export my information?

Yes. Maple Tracker generates a PDF summary of your residency timeline, your trips and your current count, in English and French.
