How It Works

Three Simple Steps

Get started in minutes and let Maple Tracker do the counting.

1

Set Up Your Profile

Enter your permanent residence date and your first arrival in Canada. If you had time as a temporary resident, Maple Tracker credits those days at 50% (up to 365 days).

2

Log Your Trips

Add any trips you've taken outside of Canada — manually or let automatic location tracking handle it for you. Every day away is tracked and deducted.

3

Track Your Progress

Watch your dashboard update in real time. You'll see your total days present, days remaining, and the exact date you'll become eligible to apply.
